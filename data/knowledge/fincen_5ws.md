# FinCEN-style narrative expectations (seed knowledge for Week 2 RAG)

title: FinCEN SAR Narrative — 5 Ws and How
keywords: fincen narrative who what when where why how regulator ready
text: |
  A complete SAR narrative should clearly answer Who, What, When, Where, Why, and How.
  Who identifies subjects and relationships. What describes instruments and activity.
  When provides chronology. Where covers locations and jurisdictions.
  Why explains why the activity is suspicious for this customer.
  How describes the mechanics of the suspected scheme.
  Narratives must be factual, chronological, and useful to law enforcement.
