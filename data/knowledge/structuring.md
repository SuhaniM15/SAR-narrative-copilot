# Structuring typology

title: Structuring (Smurfing) Red Flags
keywords: structuring smurfing threshold evasion cash deposit CTR below 10000
text: |
  Structuring involves breaking funds into amounts designed to evade currency transaction
  reporting thresholds (commonly $10,000 in the US). Multiple near-threshold cash deposits
  across branches or days, especially inconsistent with customer profile, support a
  structuring typology narrative.
